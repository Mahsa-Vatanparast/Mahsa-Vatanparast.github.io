About

I am an MD and AI researcher working at the intersection of Medical Imaging, Neuroimaging, and Trustworthy AI. My research focuses on developing AI systems that can quantify and communicate uncertainty, with the goal of making medical AI more reliable for clinical use.

Research Interests

Uncertainty Quantification & Conformal Prediction

Developing reliable uncertainty-aware AI methods for medical imaging, including voxel-wise uncertainty quantification and conformal prediction.

Medical Imaging & Neuroimaging

Artificial intelligence methods for quantitative analysis of medical and brain imaging data.

Radiogenomics

Non-invasive prediction of molecular and genetic characteristics from medical imaging.

Vision-Language & Multimodal AI

Exploring multimodal AI and vision-language models for clinically meaningful medical image understanding.
