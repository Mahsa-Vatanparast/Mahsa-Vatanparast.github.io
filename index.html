Research

I'm a physician and medical AI researcher focused on uncertainty quantification for medical imaging. My work applies conformal prediction and related methods to radiology report generation, glioma segmentation, molecular marker classification and image registration, so that deep learning models can flag low-confidence predictions and become more trustworthy in clinical settings.

This work has been recognized with the SIIM Helen and Paul Chang Foundation New Investigator Award and the ASFNR John L. Ulmer Award.
